Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an interval session?

Tap the + button on the Intervals tab. Give your interval session a name, then add intervals with custom durations, colors, and alert sounds. Tap Save when you're done.

What's the difference between Interval Sessions and Quick Timers?

Interval sessions are reusable sequences of intervals — great for structured workouts, cooking steps, or focus blocks. Quick timers are simple, one-off countdowns you can start immediately without saving.

Can I repeat an interval or an entire interval session?

Yes. Each interval has its own repeat count, and the interval session itself has a repeat count too. For example, you can set a 30-second work interval to repeat 8 times within an interval session, and run the whole interval session twice.

How do I change the alert sound?

When editing an interval, tap the Alert field. You can choose from built-in sounds, use text-to-speech to announce the interval name, or select a track from your music library.

Can I keep the screen on during playback?

Yes. Go to Settings and enable Keep Screen Awake. This prevents your device from going to sleep while an interval session is running.

How do I switch between the ring and fill playback views?

In Settings, find the Playback View Style option and choose either Ring or Fill. You can also swipe between them during playback.
